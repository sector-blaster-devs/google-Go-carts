CREDITS & LICENSES

1:87 HO SCALE · DESKTOP ROLLERCOASTER RACER

Desk Racer is an interactive real-time 3D miniature coaster time-trial simulator built for the modern web. It fuses precision rigid-body dynamics, custom raycast coaster suspension, ACES Filmic photographic rendering with Vogel-spiral depth-of-field, and procedural Web Audio synthesis on a detailed 1:87 desktop diorama.

CORE DISCIPLINES & SYSTEMS
SIMULATION & DYNAMICS
Rapier 3D Physics & Banking Math
High-speed 60 Hz physics loop, multi-point raycast wheel suspension, dynamic downforce curves, lateral drift slide mechanics, and continuous coaster spline banking.
GRAPHICS & VISUALS
Three.js Macro WebGL Pipeline
ACES Filmic tone mapping, 25-tap Golden-Angle Vogel spiral hardware-depth bokeh pass, Unreal bloom glow, dynamic tire smoke particles, and real-time 3D projected minimap radar.
AUDIO SYNTHESIS
Procedural Web Audio Engine
Multi-harmonic engine frequency synthesis, biquad resonant filter sweeps for tire screeches, curb rumble resonance, and authentic FIA Formula 1 countdown audio chimes.
SCALE & DIORAMA
1:87 Micro Scale Environment
Calibrated against realistic desk equipment: Logitech G915 mechanical keyboard, Razer mouse mat, BoYata laptop riser, Fifine AM8 mic, and warm golden-hour architectural lighting.
